      What changed on Nov 1, 2026

      • BeforeTax was removed at the register.
      • NowYou pay the full price with tax. The tax is refunded after customs confirms you are taking the goods out of Japan.
      • At the storeShow your passport and set up how you'll get the refund (for example, your credit card). Ask the store to explain its refund steps.
      • ¥5,000Minimum purchase before tax, counted per store per day. Gold and platinum bullion and tax-exempt items don't qualify.
      • 90 daysThe customs check must be done within 90 days of each purchase (up from 30). The official wording doesn't say whether the purchase day counts, so this tool shows the earlier possible date to keep you safe.
      • No sealConsumables no longer need sealed bags, but items used or eaten in Japan are not refunded.
      • Per receiptCustoms checks each receipt as a whole. Carry everything you bought.
      • RefundPaid by the store or a refund service it uses, usually after the customs check: bank transfer, card, app, or sometimes cash at the airport. Which ones are offered and when you get paid depend on the store, so ask when you buy.
